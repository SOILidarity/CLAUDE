Purpose & research questions

Purpose of the study

To describe the lived professional experiences of psychologists in Luzon on adolescent digital well-being in gaming environments.

Scope: psychologists' professional accounts, practicing in Luzon
SSOP 1

Encounter and interpret

How do they describe encountering and interpreting adolescent digital well-being in gaming?

SSOP 2

Weigh surrounding factors

How do they describe the factors that shape it?

SSOP 3

Respond in practice

(a) Benefits and concerns, (b) challenges, and (c) strategies.

Recommendations

Practice

Psychologists & counselorsAsk about gaming at intake; assess its purpose and its effect on sleep, school and relationships.
ParentsAsk why and what they play; set limits together.
SchoolsSleep and gaming psychoeducation; offline activities that build belonging.
AdolescentsUse phone well-being settings; notice when gaming displaces sleep, meals or school.

Future research

  • Broader sample: complete the intended 10 psychologists
