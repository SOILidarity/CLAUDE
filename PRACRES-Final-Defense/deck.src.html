Purpose of the study

To describe the lived professional experiences of psychologists in Luzon on adolescent digital well-being in gaming environments.

Scope: seven psychologists in Luzon · five practicing in Pampanga

Weigh surrounding factors

How do they describe the factors that shape it?

Respond in practice

(a) Benefits and concerns, (b) challenges, and (c) strategies.

LAS Findings, Themes 1.1 to 3.6.

Interpret gaming within daily life

Read gaming by its function and daily-life effects, not screen time alone.

Digital well-being is balance and control, not the absence of gaming.

Look beyond the game to the adolescent's experiences and needs.

One account differedInterviewee 4 located the risk in screen exposure itself, which she presented as her clinical hypothesis.

Recommendations

Practice

StakeholderPossible action
PractitionersAsk purpose; assess sleep, school, relationships and routines.
FamiliesAsk what play provides; agree on workable limits together.
SchoolsOffer sleep psychoeducation and offline opportunities for belonging.
AdolescentsNotice when play displaces sleep, meals, self-care or school.

Drawn from the psychologists' accounts.

Future research

  • Wider sample
    More of Luzon; adolescents and parents
  • Local culture
    Kapampangan and other local meanings of gaming
  • Change over time
    Examine stress, gaming and sleep longitudinally
  • Emerging concerns
    Doomscrolling, social media; test screen-exposure claims separately
